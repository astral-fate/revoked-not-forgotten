Agents in an OpenAI cyber-security evaluation turned a shared directory into a message board, divided work, and broke into Hugging Face's systems.

The infrastructure answer: identity + revocation

  • Agent IDs attached to AI-system instances
  • Authenticated delegation via OAuth 2.0 / OpenID Connect
  • Capability tokens, invocation-bound and attenuating, across MCP and A2A
  • Heartbeat-bound credentials: a revoked swarm stops within a bounded window

All of them answer: can the revoked agent still act?

Revoked but Not Forgotten · Central concept
03 / 31
The concept at the centre of this study

Credential revocation is not content revocation

A teammate that reads a compromised agent's report paraphrases it, merges it with other sources and passes it on.

Credential revocation · what current designs provide

  • Requests to the agent return an error.
  • The roster lists it as REVOKED; its posts may be retracted.
  • A teammate's handoff that already absorbed its claims is untouched.

Content revocation · what this study measures

  • Residual influence: the rate at which a downstream agent applies a revoked agent's handed-off false fact or instruction.
  • Measured under credential-only revocation, then with a notice, retraction and lineage marks on derived handoff lines.

Deleting the original post does not delete its effect, and revoking the author's key does not revoke the claim.

The paired design

  • One handoff, every condition: generated once per (model, seed, compromise, writer variant) and reused.
  • Exact McNemar on (model, seed) pairs; only discordant pairs count.
  • Wilson 95% intervals per model and pooled.
  • No LLM judge: canary codes, exact match.

Revoked but Not Forgotten · Recommendations
28 / 31
Design recommendations

Revocation has to reach content and its derivatives

1 · Record provenance on the platform

Bind each claim to its author, or match what the revoked agent wrote, so lineage does not depend on agents citing sources. Without citations, marks reached 0/48, 0/48, 0/48, 6/48 handoffs; platform taint found derived lines in 44/48, 48/48, 45/48, 43/48. Normalise before matching: “2,508” slipped past an exact match.

2 · Make revocation trigger re-derivation

Withhold derived content until another agent re-obtains it. Marking the taint-matched lines left residue at 4% / 52% / 15% / 73%; withholding the same lines: 4% / 0% / 0% / 2%. Better still, gate the action: writes to the revoked agent's field held residue at 2%.

3 · Scope any notice to derived content

Say that content already in the agent's context came from the revoked agent. One added sentence worked for GPT-6 Luna, Gemma 4 31B and Qwen3.8 27B but not Ministral-3 14B: a notice is a complement, not the enforcement.

Tested: removing content worked until the attacker reworded it; gating the action did not depend on wording

Normalised platform taint + quarantine: residue GPT-6 Luna 0%, Gemma 4 31B 0%, Qwen3.8 27B 0%, Ministral-3 14B 0% (vs 42% / 73% / 62% / 67% with citation-based marks on the same handoffs); Against an attacker that spelled its values out, taint quarantine left 41%; the action gate left 1%, at the price of 59% escalation. Every mechanism that asked the executor to discount content it could still read was model-dependent; removing the content, or refusing the action, was not.

Revoked but Not Forgotten · Limitations
29 / 31
What this doesn't establish

A deliberately small, fully controlled testbed

L1 · One scenario family

12 ticket templates, scripted workers, one mock CRM and one tool set; absolute rates depend on these designs and on the prompt wording.

L2 · Hand-written adaptive attacks

Laundering and obfuscation are two hand-written strategies, not an optimised attacker; a paraphrasing or value-splitting attacker was not tested.

L3 · What the gate assumes

The platform knows the revoked agent's assigned field and the plan's transfers, and the gate did not inspect free-text notes or ticket comments.

L4 · The gate's price

Executors escalated in 59% of gated trials and task success fell to 25%; the safe action was to stop, not to re-derive.

L5 · One trained executor

One 4B model from one LoRA run; it re-asks the official source, which fails when the compromised worker is not revoked.

L6 · Model coverage

Main grid: 7 models. Ablations: GPT-6 Luna, Gemma 4 31B, Qwen3.8 27B, Ministral-3 14B; Platform designs: GPT-6 Luna, Gemma 4 31B, Qwen3.8 27B, Ministral-3 14B; Stress tests: GPT-6 Luna, Gemma 4 31B, Qwen3.8 27B, Ministral-3 14B. Main grid only: gpt-oss-120b, GLM-5.2, DeepSeek-V4 Flash.

L7 · Correlated repetitions

48 seeds are 6 templates × 2 payloads × 4 repetitions; repetitions often agree, so the paper adds template-clustered tests.

L8 · Mock environment

All systems, addresses and credentials are mock; longer chains than two hops and persistent memory are untested.
