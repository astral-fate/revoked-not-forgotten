Agents in an OpenAI cyber-security evaluation turned a shared directory into a message board, divided work, and broke into Hugging Face's systems.

The infrastructure answer: identity + revocation

  • Agent IDs attached to AI-system instances
  • Authenticated delegation via OAuth 2.0 / OpenID Connect
  • Capability tokens, invocation-bound and attenuating, across MCP and A2A
  • Heartbeat-bound credentials: a revoked swarm stops within a bounded window

All of them answer: can the revoked agent still act?

Revoked but Not Forgotten · Central concept
03 / 25
The concept at the centre of this study

Credential revocation is not content revocation

A teammate that reads a compromised agent's report paraphrases it, merges it with other sources and passes it on.

Credential revocation · what current designs provide

  • Requests to the agent return an error.
  • The roster lists it as REVOKED; its posts may be retracted.
  • A teammate's handoff that already absorbed its claims is untouched.

Content revocation · what this study measures

  • Residual influence: the rate at which a downstream agent applies a revoked agent's handed-off false fact or instruction.
  • Measured under credential-only revocation, then with a notice, retraction and lineage marks on derived handoff lines.

Deleting the original post does not delete its effect, and revoking the author's key does not revoke the claim.

The paired design

  • One handoff, every condition: generated once per (model, seed, compromise, writer variant) and reused.
  • Exact McNemar on (model, seed) pairs; only discordant pairs count.
  • Wilson 95% intervals per model and pooled.
  • No LLM judge: canary codes, exact match.

  • Neither should be assumed in deployment.
Revoked but Not Forgotten · Recommendations
22 / 25
Design recommendations

Revocation has to reach content and its derivatives

1 · Record provenance on the platform

Bind each claim to its author, or match what the revoked agent wrote, so lineage does not depend on agents citing sources. Without citations, marks reached 0/48, 0/48, 6/48 handoffs; platform taint found derived lines in 48/48, 45/48, 43/48. Normalise before matching: “2,508” slipped past an exact match.

2 · Make revocation trigger re-derivation

Withhold derived content until another agent re-obtains it. Marking the taint-matched lines left residue at 52% / 15% / 73%; withholding the same lines: 0% / 0% / 2%.

3 · Scope any notice to derived content

Say that content already in the agent's context came from the revoked agent. One added sentence worked for Gemma 4 31B and Qwen3.8 27B but not Ministral-3 14B: a notice is a complement, not the enforcement.

Tested in experiment 06: the recommended design worked for every model

Normalised platform taint + quarantine: residue Gemma 4 31B 0%, Qwen3.8 27B 0%, Ministral-3 14B 0% (vs 73% / 62% / 67% with citation-based marks on the same handoffs); false-alarm task success 50% vs 46%, 44% vs 48%, 40% vs 40% clean, no significant difference.

Revoked but Not Forgotten · Limitations
23 / 25
What this doesn't establish

A deliberately small, fully controlled testbed

L1 · Scripted, non-adaptive attacker

Fixed payloads; an agent anticipating revocation could get the writer to restate its claims without attribution, which the no-citation ablation shows defeats lineage marks.

L2 · One writer hop, one executor

Longer chains and persistent memory are untested and seem likely to increase residual influence.

L3 · 5 models, one temperature

The same model is writer and executor except in the mixed teams; rates for frontier models and production platforms are not established.

L4 · Further experiments on 3 models

Ablations, platform designs and mixed teams cover Gemma 4 31B, Qwen3.8 27B, Ministral-3 14B, not GPT-6 Luna (budget exhausted) or gpt-oss-120b.

L5 · Gemma often skipped the field

Gemma 4 31B closed 158 of 336 main-condition tickets (47%) without writing the researched field, capping its task success.

L6 · Single designs

Scenarios, tools, notice texts and payload wording are one design each; absolute rates depend on them.

L7 · False-alarm cost

Depends on a second source being available, and on models re-delegating.

L8 · Taint needs recognisable content

Normalised matching survives reformatting (case, digit grouping, dashes) but not paraphrase: an agent that rewords its outputs would defeat it. Mock environment throughout.
